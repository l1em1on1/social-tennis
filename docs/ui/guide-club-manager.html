Running the club

Share the join link, look after who’s in, and hand leagues to people who’ll run them. Follow Morgan on the club’s first day.

Open the club

  1. 1 Everything is in Manage

    An Admin of the app makes you a manager of your club. Manage then shows the club: its leagues, its link, and who’s in it. If you manage several clubs, each has its own card.

  2. 2 Turn on the join link

    Each club has one link to ask to join. It’s off until you turn it on.

  3. 3 Share it

    Copy the link into the club’s chat or an email. Opening it only lets someone ask: you still decide. Reset link makes a new one and stops the old, and Turn off stops it altogether.

Who is in

  1. 4 Let people in

    Under Asking to join, press the tick to approve or the cross to decline.

  2. 5 Or add someone yourself

    Type their email address. With a name, that player is in now, and can be put in leagues before they ever sign in. Use a parent’s address for a child.

  3. 6 They’re in

    The app doesn’t email them, so let them know: they sign in with that address.

  4. 7 Pre-approve an address

    Without a name, the address is pre-approved: whoever signs in with it is let in with no request. The cross takes it back.

  5. 8 See your members

    Members are grouped by who signs in for them, so a parent and their children sit together. Not signed in yet marks anyone you added who hasn’t come along.

  6. 9 Remove a member

    Press the bin beside a name, then Remove from club. They can ask to join again.

Share the work

  1. 10 Choose a League manager

    Press the three dots beside someone’s email address.

  2. 11 Make them a League manager

    A League manager creates leagues at your club and runs those. They can’t manage the club or its members.

  3. 12 See who helps, and take it back

    Managers lists everyone with a role. The cross takes the League manager role away; the leagues they run stay theirs until you take those too.

The club’s leagues

  1. 13 Every league is yours to run

    You see all the club’s leagues, and can do in each what its League manager can. Press one to open it, or New league to create one.

  2. 14 Give a league to someone

    At the foot of a league’s page, pick a member under Give this league to. They run that league and nothing else. The cross takes it back. A league given to nobody is run by the club’s managers.

Good to know

How do I run a league?
The same way a League manager does: see their guide.
Who can be a League manager?
Anyone with a player in your club.
Who makes club managers?
An Admin of the app, for each club.
What do members see of who manages?
A league’s players see who runs it, by name and never by email address.
Who sees the members’ email addresses?
The club’s managers, for their own club; a League manager, for players in their leagues or asking to join one; and the app’s Admins.
